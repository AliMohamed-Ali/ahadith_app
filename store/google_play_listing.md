## Store listing — English (en-US)

**App name** (27/30)
Hadith Kids – أحاديث الصغار

**Short description** (76/80)
Learn the Prophet’s hadiths through stories, games and stars — made for kids

**Full description**
Hadith Kids is a fun journey where children learn and memorize the hadiths of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ step by step, through illustrated stories, games and rewards.

🌊 The river of stations
Children travel along a river where every station is a new hadith. Levels grow from short, easy hadiths up to the complete Forty Hadith of an-Nawawi.

📖 A story for every hadith
Each hadith comes with a short illustrated story that explains its meaning, plus audio to listen and repeat.

🎮 Games that build memory
Fish, bubbles and the train: kids complete the hadith's words and strengthen their memory while they play.

⭐ Stars and rewards
Regular stars for every game won, and royal stars 👑 for every level completed. Stars unlock new colors and decorations for the child's own lantern.

🌳 Good Deeds Garden
Every game won plants a seedling in the child's garden, which grows into a fruit tree.

✅ Real-life missions
Each hadith comes with a practical mission for the child's day, confirmed by a parent.

🎬 Essentials every Muslim child should know
Short video and question sections on the basics of belief and worship, made for children.

🏆 Leaderboard (optional)
With a parent's approval, a child can appear on the leaderboard with only a first name and cartoon character.

👨‍👩‍👧 Safe for kids
• No ads
• No email or phone sign-up
• The child's photo and progress stay on the device
• Sharing and deletion are protected by a parent gate
• Arabic and English

Download Hadith Kids and help your child grow up with the Sunnah.

## App content answers

**Ads:** No, the app does not contain ads.

**App access:** All functionality is available without special access.

**Target audience:** Ages 5 and under, 6–8, 9–12 (choose the ones you want). The app is **not** designed to appeal to older audiences only, so it joins the Families program.

**Content rating questionnaire:** Category *All Other App Types*. Answer **No** to everything (violence, fear, sexuality, language, controlled substances, gambling, user interaction, location sharing, digital purchases, web browser) except **Yes** to "primarily a news or educational product". The leaderboard shows only a nickname, a preset cartoon character and a governorate chosen from a list; children can't send messages to each other.

**News app:** No. **Government app:** No. **Financial features:** None. **Health:** No.

### Data safety

- Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? **Yes**
- Is all user data encrypted in transit? **Yes**
- Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted? **Yes** (in-app "Delete all data" + email)
- Shared with third parties: **No** for all types (Firebase and Cloudinary are service providers, which Play doesn't count as sharing)

| Data type | Collected | Required / Optional | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal info → Name (nickname on leaderboard) | Yes | Optional | App functionality |
| Location → Approximate location (governorate/area picked by the parent) | Yes | Optional | App functionality |
| App activity → Other actions (star counts on leaderboard) | Yes | Optional | App functionality |
| Device or other IDs (anonymous Firebase ID, notification token) | Yes | Required | App functionality |

Photos, contacts, precise location, email, phone and analytics: **not collected**. The profile photo is processed only on the device, which Play doesn't count as collection.
